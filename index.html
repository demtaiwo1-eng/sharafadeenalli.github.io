CAMPTECH OYO
Public information · Media intelligence · Field operations
Foundation preview
Oyo State · Nigeria

One trusted place for public information and coordinated action.

CampTech is a proposed information and operations platform for collecting public updates, organising field reports, coordinating events and publishing verified information transparently.

Data status: This is the foundation interface. Live source feeds, user accounts, reporting APIs and operational data have not yet been connected. No figures shown here are presented as live results.
01 · Public

Public information hub

Publish dated announcements, explainers, event information and verified updates with clear source attribution.

02 · Monitoring

Open-source media monitoring

Organise publicly available news and public posts by keyword, date, source and topic. Preserve links and collection timestamps.

03 · Situation room

Operations dashboard

Track projects, tasks, milestones, assigned teams and issue status with role-based access and audit history.

04 · Field

Ward and field reporting

Submit structured observations, event reports and service issues. Protect personal data and separate allegations from verified facts.

05 · Events

Events and stakeholders

Coordinate schedules, venues, logistics, vendors, attendance counts and post-event reports.

06 · Insights

Reports and transparency

Generate aggregate trend summaries, source coverage, project progress and downloadable public reports.
